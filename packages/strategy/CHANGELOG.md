# Changelog

## 0.1.0 - 2026-10-02

- Add independently installable daily strategy research with buy-and-hold, funded DCA and simple moving-average rules.
- Ship one pure Node/browser engine with next-observation execution, modeled fees and adverse slippage, positive cash contributions, flow-neutral returns and drawdown, and an identical-flow buy-and-hold benchmark.
- Include an offline CLI with exact input hashes, a synthetic worked example and regression cases for leakage, timing, accounting and invalid evidence.
- Document aggregate-price limitations and a research workflow for unseen periods and cost sensitivity. No trading or scheduler is bundled.
